Welcome to the first development update for **Fallen Zenith**.

This space will collect progress reports, design notes, and glimpses into the world taking shape behind the game.

## What comes next

Development is still at an early stage, but the foundations are being laid one piece at a time:

- Building the world and its story
- Defining its characters
- Exploring the visual identity
- Turning ideas into playable systems

The journey has only just begun.
